About

I own features end to end

Four years of building products that real people use — including the e-commerce platform behind the largest computer hardware retailer in Egypt.

Most engineers hand off at a boundary — design to frontend, frontend to API, API to deploy. I'd rather hold the whole thread. At Startup Confidential I owned both the frontend and the backend architecture of a social platform, then built its React Native companion app. I write the Next.js interface and the Go service it talks to, then containerize both and put them on a Linux box.

I started freelancing in 2022 while studying computer science, and I still keep my own product running: Qarenly, a search engine over product listings pulled from stores across Egypt.
